### Main Viewport Chart Stacking (Equal Large Sizes & Clean Dark Theme)
The main screen MUST be dominated by 3 Large, Equal-Sized, Fully Synchronized Candlestick Charts stacked vertically (Top to Bottom):
1. **Chart 1: Primary Price Action Chart** (Candlestick format + Volume Profile + Liquidity Lines).
2. **Chart 2: Cumulative Price Pressure Chart** (MUST be rendered as a CANDLESTICK CHART instead of a single line, taking full visual width & large height).
3. **Chart 3: Cumulative Sub-Bar Direction Balance Chart** (MUST also be rendered as a CANDLESTICK CHART with OHLC structure, large height).

#### Strict UI Specifications for Charts:
- **Design & Aesthetics:** High contrast dark theme (Deep Charcoal `#0D1117` background, Neon Green `#00F3B2` for Bullish candles, Neon Pink/Red `#FF0055` for Bearish candles). Clean gridlines, zero clutter, high readability.
- **Equal Heights:** Charts 1, 2, and 3 must share equal panel space (e.g., 33% viewport height each) or allow collapsible full-height viewing.
- **Synchronization:** Moving crosshairs, Panning (dragging left/right), and Zooming (scroll in/out) MUST be 100% time-synchronized across all 3 charts simultaneously.
- **Historical Testing & Navigation:** Enable smooth horizontal scrolling/swiping so the user can scroll back through 1000+ historical candles to backtest orderflow divergence signals seamlessly.
- **Timestamp Labels:** Precise date and time labels on the shared X-axis for every single candle.

## ⚡ CORE QUANT & ORDERFLOW FEATURES TO IMPLEMENT

### 1. Direct Zero-Delay Binance WebSocket Engine
- Establish direct WS connections to Binance Futures (`wss://fstream.binance.com/ws`).
- Stream live trade ticks (`aggTrade`), depth levels (`depth20@100ms`), klines (`kline_1m`), and Open Interest (`forceOrder` / OI endpoints) for the currently selected coin.

### 2. Multi-Chart Candlestick Engine
- **Price Chart:** standard OHLC candles.
- **Cumulative Price Pressure Candlestick:** Aggregate delta pressure per time bucket into Open, High, Low, Close (OHLC) values to render real candles representing orderflow pressure.
- **Cumulative Sub-Bar Direction Balance Candlestick:** Calculate directional micro-imbalance per bar and plot as full-featured OHLC candles.

### 3. Divergence Detection Engine (Price vs. Pressure & Direction Balance)
- Algorithmically scan every candle for:
  - **Bullish Divergence:** Price makes Lower Lows, but Price Pressure OR Sub-Bar Direction Balance makes Higher Lows.
  - **Bearish Divergence:** Price makes Higher Highs, but Price Pressure OR Sub-Bar Direction Balance makes Lower Highs.
- **Visual Highlights:** Draw glowing connecting trendlines directly on the candles where divergence occurs across Chart 1, Chart 2, and Chart 3.
- **Alert Banner:** Show a non-intrusive animated toast alert: *"🔥 Bullish/Bearish Divergence Detected on Chart 2/3!"*.

### 4. Direct Chart Liquidity Heatmap & Wall Lines
- Do NOT isolate liquidity into a tiny bottom widget. Draw thick horizontal glowing heatmap lines directly on Chart 1 (Price Chart) at major limit order clusters (Swing Highs/Lows and DOM limit walls).
- Annotate each line with exact liquidity size (e.g., `$2.5M Liquidity Wall at 81,200`).

### 5. Dynamic Volume Profile, POC & VWAP
- Overlay Volume Profile on Chart 1 showing Point of Control (POC - highlighted in Yellow), High Volume Nodes (HVN), and Low Volume Nodes (LVN).
- Plot Session VWAP with +1, +2, -1, -2 Standard Deviation bands directly on Chart 1.

### 6. Whale Activity, Delta Absorption & Liquidity Sweeps
- Detect aggressive volume spikes (>2.5 Standard Deviations above normal).
- Identify Passive Absorption (high aggressive delta with zero price movement). Highlight the absorption candle with a pulsing aura effect.
- Detect Liquidity Sweeps (wick sweeps above key swing level with close inside range) and mark them with a "SWEEP" label.

### 7. Interactive Risk-to-Reward Calculator Tool
- Interactive drawing tool allowing traders to place Entry, Stop-Loss, and Take-Profit lines on the price chart with auto-calculated R:R ratios (1:2, 1:3).

### 8. Orderflow Signals & Metrics Floating Bar
- Include clean top/side stats cards for:
  - Real-time Open Interest (OI) Pulse.
  - Live Binance Futures Funding Rate Gauge.
  - Stacked Imbalances (>300% buy/sell imbalance across 3 consecutive levels).
